# Ra'am (United Arab List, רע"ם) MKs and the 7 October 2023 attack — record of statements, 7 Oct 2023 – 18 Nov 2026

Research notes. Conventions:
- "VERIFIED (page read)" = I opened the page and the quote/date was on it. "SNIPPET ONLY" = seen only in a search-result summary, page not opened or blocked (Kan, Channel 14, Makor Rishon, Israel Hayom returned HTTP 403). Treat SNIPPET ONLY items as leads, not established facts.
- Hebrew quotes are as printed by the outlet. Several outlets printed Hebrew translations of statements originally made in English (CNN) or Arabic (Radio al-Nas); the original-language wording was not retrieved in those cases and this is marked.
- Context for the period: the 25th Knesset faction had five MKs for the whole period (Mansour Abbas, Walid Taha, Waleed Alhwashla, Iman Khatib-Yassin, Yasser Hujirat). I found no report that anyone entered or left the faction during the 25th Knesset. An election was held on 27 Oct 2026; Ra'am won 8 seats, so statements after that date belong to the 26th Knesset period (noted where relevant).

## 1. What did each MK say on 7 October 2023 itself and in the first days and weeks after?

### Takeaway
Only Mansour Abbas has a documented, dated statement on 7 October 2023 itself (a Hebrew X post calling the events "regrettable, tragic and reprehensible" and urging restraint, without naming Hamas), followed on 10 Oct by a call to "the Palestinian factions in Gaza" to free captive women, children and elderly. For Taha, Alhwashla and Hujirat I found no dated first-weeks statement about the attack itself; Khatib-Yassin's first documented statement is the 5 Nov 2023 Knesset Channel interview (section 3).

Critics (Mida, Channel 14) emphasize this; supporters and The Seventh Eye emphasize that he condemned at once.

## 2. Mansour Abbas: condemnation, hostages, Hamas, Arabic vs Hebrew, evolution 2024–2026

### Takeaway
Abbas has consistently and repeatedly condemned the 7 October acts (as "inhuman", "crimes", contrary to Islam), called for release of hostages and for Palestinian armed groups to disarm; he has just as consistently declined, in 2025 and 2026, to say on the record that Hamas is a terrorist organization or should be destroyed, citing his role as a "bridge" and fear for his and his family's safety. In Nov 2026 Ynet reported he was willing in principle to make the designation in exchange for coalition partnership; he did not deny it in Hebrew at his faction meeting (10 Nov) and denied it in Arabic on Hala TV/Panet (11 Nov).

## 3. Iman Khatib-Yassin: the November 2023 remarks, apology, resignation demand, outcome

### Takeaway
On 5 Nov 2023, on the Knesset Channel, Khatib-Yassin — who had not watched the IDF film — said "they didn't slaughter babies, at least from what was in the film, and they didn't rape women"; she apologized in writing the same evening, before the Ethics Committee on 7 Nov and from the plenum podium on 13 Nov. Abbas demanded she resign immediately; she did not resign, remained a Ra'am MK, was suspended by the Ethics Committee (unanimously) for one month with two weeks' pay docked, and was placed 5th on Ra'am's 2026 list and re-elected.

### Cited Findings
- 5 Nov 2023, Knesset Channel interview (Hebrew). Quote as recorded in the Ethics Committee decision: "לא שחטו תינוקות, לפחות ממה שהיה בסרט, ולא אנסו נשים". The decision records that the interviewer gave her several chances to correct herself and she maintained that, based on the film shown to MKs and on conversations with colleagues who watched it, there was no evidence that babies were massacred and women raped; and that she also said the 7 Oct massacre was harsh, "לא אנושי", contrary to Islam, "לא הדרך לפתור סכסוך מתמשך", and that one cannot ignore "ממה שהיה לפני או שהיה אחרי". VERIFIED (primary document, PDF text extracted) — [Knesset Ethics Committee Decision 18/25, 15 Nov 2023 (copy hosted by Adalah)](https://www.adalah.org/uploads/uploads/Khatib-Yassin_Suspension_15_Nov_2023.pdf); clip: [Knesset Channel](https://www.knesset.tv/tv-shows/hazira-hamerkazit/article/61440/).
- Additional interview wording: "לו היו רואים גם אונס או 'שחיטת תינוקות' - זה היה מביש" — [N12/Mako, 5 Nov 2023](https://www.mako.co.il/news-politics/2023_q4/Article-eaced31d0cf9b81027.htm); English: "If it happened, it's shameful… If it had happened," it would have been included; she had not watched because it would "only cause more pain"; what happened on 7 Oct was inhumane and against Islam but "we can't ignore everything that came before, or what came after" — [Times of Israel, 5 Nov 2023](https://www.timesofisrael.com/raam-party-chief-calls-on-mk-to-resign-for-claiming-hamas-didnt-slaughter-babies/). On not watching: "לא יכולה לצפות, שמעתי ממקור ראשון" — [Maariv, 8 Nov 2023](https://www.maariv.co.il/news/politics/article-1050671).

Ra'am leadership told her she had no place on the party's Knesset list and demanded she resign immediately — [N12/Mako](https://www.mako.co.il/news-politics/2023_q4/Article-eaced31d0cf9b81027.htm); Abbas said he "was shocked" — [Times of Israel](https://www.timesofisrael.com/raam-party-chief-calls-on-mk-to-resign-for-claiming-hamas-didnt-slaughter-babies/). 10 Nov 2023, Channel 12: "אני עומד מאחורי הדברים שלי, שאין מקום לא מוסרית, לא דתית ולא ציבורית לדבר בשפה שיכולה להישמע כהכחשה" — [N12/Mako](https://www.mako.co.il/news-politics/2023_q4/Article-57454f271e9bb81027.htm).

"Abbas pressured her to resign her Knesset seat. ### Inferences
- Self-contradiction on record: 5 Nov 2023 interview ("didn't slaughter babies… didn't rape women") vs. same-evening written apology, 7 Nov committee statement (believes the reported atrocities occurred; Hamas committed "acts of terror") and 13 Nov plenum speech (acts with "no justification"; attributes misunderstanding partly to translation).
- Abbas's 5 Nov 2023 statement ("no place in our ranks") was not followed by her removal from the faction or the list; she ran again in 2026 on a realistic slot.

### Gaps
- No evidence found of any faction-level sanction by Ra'am. Not found ≠ none imposed.
- No statements by her about 7 October from 2024–2026 were found.
- Full interview transcript and full text of her 13 Nov 2023 plenum speech (Knesset protocol) not retrieved.
