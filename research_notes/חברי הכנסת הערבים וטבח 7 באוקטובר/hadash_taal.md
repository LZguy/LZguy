# Hadash-Ta'al (חד"ש-תע"ל) MKs of the 25th Knesset and the 7 October 2023 attack: dated record, 7 Oct 2023 to 18 Nov 2026

Research notes. Scope: Ayman Odeh, Ahmad Tibi, Aida Touma-Sliman, Ofer Cassif (the list's Jewish MK), Youssef Atauna, Samir Bin Said (entered June 2025 by rotation).

Reading conventions used throughout:
- "Verified" = I opened the page and the quote is in it. "Snippet only" = seen only in a search-result excerpt; the page itself returned 403 or was not opened. Treat snippet-only items as leads, not as confirmed quotes.
- Hebrew quotes are as printed by the cited outlet. Several were originally spoken in Arabic or English and are the outlet's Hebrew rendering; I mark this where known. I did not open any primary X/Facebook post or Knesset plenum protocol, so every quote below is as relayed by a secondary outlet.
- Outlet leanings are flagged where relevant: Arutz Sheva/INN, Srugim, Kipa, Makor Rishon, Channel 14, JNS, Kikar HaShabbat, Behadrei Haredim = right-wing/religious; Zo Haderekh, maki.org.il, People's Democracy, Democracy Now = Communist/left; The Seventh Eye (העין השביעית), Local Call (שיחה מקומית) = left-liberal media criticism; Times of Israel, Ynet, Maariv, Walla, Kan, AP, AFP = mainstream.
- Background that changed in 2026: the 25th Knesset dissolved and an election was held on 27 October 2026. Hadash, Ta'al and Balad ran as a revived Joint List chaired by Yousef Jabareen, which won 11 seats. Odeh and Touma-Sliman did not run. [Times of Israel, 1 Nov 2026](https://www.timesofisrael.com/tibi-says-joint-list-ready-to-back-anti-netanyahu-government-from-the-outside/)

## 1. The Hadash / Hadash-Ta'al statement of 7-8 October 2023: wording, what it did and did not say, reactions

### Takeaway
A Hadash-Maki (Communist Party) statement condemned "any harm to innocent civilians" and sent condolences to "Arabs and Jews alike", while placing "full responsibility" for the escalation on the "fascist right-wing government" and the occupation; as relayed, it did not name Hamas as perpetrator and did not mention the hostages. I could not obtain the original Hebrew or Arabic full text, and sources disagree on whether it was issued on 7 or 8 October.

### Cited Findings
- English rendering of the Communist Party of Israel and Hadash statement, dated 8 October: it expresses "an unequivocal condemnation of any harm to innocent civilians", sends condolences to families of victims "Arabs and Jews alike", puts "the full responsibility" for the "sharp and dangerous escalation" on the "fascist right-wing government", blames the occupation policy, calls for international intervention and a political solution, and warns of revenge attacks on Arab citizens. The statement text does not name Hamas and does not mention hostages (the word "Hamas" appears only in the outlet's own introduction). Communist-party outlet; secondary English translation. — [People's Democracy (CPI-M weekly), 22 Oct 2023](https://peoplesdemocracy.in/2023/1022_pd/%E2%80%9Cnetanyahu%E2%80%99s-govt-bears-full-responsibility-escalation%E2%80%9D)
- Hebrew fragment of the same statement, attributed to "סיעת חד"ש ומפלגת מק"י": "חוזרים ומשמיעים גינוי חד-משמעי לכל פגיעה באזרחים חפים מפשע" ("we repeat and voice an unequivocal condemnation of any harm to innocent civilians"). — [The Seventh Eye, Oren Persico, 26 Dec 2023](https://www.the7eye.org.il/505126)
- Local Call reported on 15 Oct 2023 that Hadash "issued a statement today (Sunday) condemning the harm to civilians in the Gaza envelope" (i.e. dates it to Sunday 8 October), without quoting it. — [Local Call / שיחה מקומית, Baker Zoabi, 15 Oct 2023](https://www.mekomit.co.il/מאז-המשטר-הצבאי-הערבים-בישראל-לא-פחדו/)
- Reaction, 7 October: per The Seventh Eye, Channel 12 / Yedioth journalist Amit Segal complained on 7 October, before the end of Shabbat, that Tibi and Odeh had not condemned the Hamas massacre; the article says he removed the post after Tibi objected and clarified that their principled position is against harm to civilians whoever they are. This is The Seventh Eye's description; I did not see Segal's post or Tibi's reply. — [The Seventh Eye, 26 Dec 2023](https://www.the7eye.org.il/505126)
- The Seventh Eye's overall claim (made in a piece produced with the Sikkuy-Aufoq NGO and Yifat media research) is that Arab politicians condemned the massacre and Israeli mainstream media largely ignored those condemnations. Channel 14's Shai Golan is cited there as saying on 15 Oct 2023 that Tibi "kept silent" while Mansour Abbas condemned. — [The Seventh Eye, 26 Dec 2023](https://www.the7eye.org.il/505126)
- Cassif, speaking for the party on Al Jazeera English on 8 Oct 2023: "We condemn and oppose any assault on innocent civilians", adding "we oppose any assault on Palestinian civilians as well"; called the attacks "terrible incidents" to be understood in the context of "the ongoing occupation". — [Al Jazeera, 8 Oct 2023](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2023/10/8/israeli-lawmaker-blames-pogroms-against-palestinians-for-terrible-attacks)

### Inferences
- On the available text, the statement is a "qualified" type: a general condemnation of harm to civilians plus attribution of responsibility to the Israeli government and the occupation, with no named perpetrator. That is a description of the wording, not a judgement of intent.
- "Hadash-Ta'al faction statement" and "Hadash-Maki statement" may be two different documents. Every text I found is attributed to Hadash and Maki, not to the joint Hadash-Ta'al Knesset faction.

### Gaps
- Original Hebrew/Arabic full text not found (Zo Haderekh, Al-Ittihad and the parties' social accounts were not reachable through search). Exact date (7 vs 8 Oct) unresolved.
- No separate Ta'al party statement of 7 October found.
- No mainstream-outlet report quoting the statement in full, and no contemporaneous government or coalition reaction specifically to this statement was found.

## 2. Ayman Odeh (Hadash chair until May 2026; did not run in 2026)

### Takeaway
Odeh's recorded statements repeatedly condemn the killing and abduction of civilians on 7 October ("nothing in the world, not even the cursed occupation, justifies harming innocents") and from November 2023 he used the word "massacre"; in the same period he affirmed "the right of every occupied people to struggle against the occupier", said "Gaza has won", and in a February 2026 Channel 12 interview did not answer yes or no when asked whether Hamas is a terrorist organization. Two attempts to sanction him over his January 2025 "hostages and prisoners" post ended with the expulsion motion failing in the plenum, 73-15, on 14 July 2025.

### Cited Findings
**October-November 2023**
- 10 Oct 2023, X/Twitter (as quoted by The Seventh Eye): "אסור בתכלית האיסור לקבל כל פגיעה באזרחים חפים מפשע, ואני מגנה בכל תוקף את הקריאה של הנהגת חמאס לציבור הערבי להצטרף למאבק נגד ישראל" ("It is absolutely forbidden to accept any harm to innocent civilians, and I strongly condemn the call of the Hamas leadership to the Arab public to join the struggle against Israel"). Note: the condemnation of Hamas by name here concerns its call to Arab citizens, not the attack itself. — [The Seventh Eye, 26 Dec 2023](https://www.the7eye.org.il/505126)
- 11 Oct 2023 (reported), interview with Hala TV (Arabic-language): "It is absolutely forbidden to accept any attacks on the innocent"; "we must remove all citizens from the circle of conflict and war, Palestinians and Israelis alike"; "the occupation and its continuation is the basic problem that must end in order to establish peace". Same report: he called on the government to release all Palestinian prisoners and move immediately to a prisoner exchange. — [maki.org.il (Communist Party of Israel, English), 11 Oct 2023](https://maki.org.il/en/?p=31257)
- 11 Oct 2023, X/Twitter: "שום דבר בעולם לא מצדיק פגיעה בחפים מפשע, לא בדרום ולא בעזה" ("Nothing in the world justifies harming innocents, not in the south and not in Gaza"). — [The Seventh Eye, 26 Dec 2023](https://www.the7eye.org.il/505126)
- 12 Oct 2023, Knesset plenum: "אין שום דבר בעולם, ולא הכיבוש הארור, שמצדיק פגיעה בחפים מפשע" ("There is nothing in the world, not the cursed occupation either, that justifies harming innocents"); "תמיד התנגדתי ואמשיך להתנגד בכל רמ"ח אבריי לפגיעה באזרחים". — [The Seventh Eye, 26 Dec 2023](https://www.the7eye.org.il/505126)
- Knesset speech, October 2023 (date not given): "העם שלנו סבל הרבה, מבחינה מוסרית ומדינית אסור לפגוע באזרח או כל אדם חף מפשע"; "ניהלנו מאבק מכובד ואחראי מאז 1948, וכך הוא חייב להישאר". — [Local Call, 15 Oct 2023](https://www.mekomit.co.il/מאז-המשטר-הצבאי-הערבים-בישראל-לא-פחדו/)
- Attribution conflict: the sentence "רצח נשים וילדים וקשישים וזוועות נגד אזרחים שהיו בדרום ראויים להוקעה ללא היסוס" is attributed to Odeh (plenum, 12 Oct) in my reading of [The Seventh Eye](https://www.the7eye.org.il/505126) but to Tibi by [Local Call](https://www.mekomit.co.il/מאז-המשטר-הצבאי-הערבים-בישראל-לא-פחדו/) and [Maariv, 2 Nov 2023](https://www.maariv.co.il/news/politics/article-1049374). Two sources against one favour Tibi; not checked against the Knesset protocol.
- Late Oct 2023: no Hadash-Ta'al MK attended the first Knesset screening of the IDF compilation of 7 October footage; Odeh and Tibi were among the first to register for the additional screening (Kan report, snippet only). — [Kan, 2023](https://kan.org.il/content/kan-news/local/599959/)
- 15 Nov 2023, Radio Nas (Arabic-language radio; Hebrew rendering by Srugim), after watching the footage: "מראים מזוויעים שאין לתאר אותם, אני לא יכול לקבל שבשם העם הפלסטיני הורגים חפים מפשע בצורה כזאת" ("Horrifying sights that cannot be described; I cannot accept that in the name of the Palestinian people innocents are killed this way"); "איך אפשר להתגאות במורשת של האסלאם, שמצווה עלינו בעת מלחמה לא לפגוע בתינוקות ונשים ואפילו עצים, ואז לעשות דברים כאלה?". Srugim adds, in its own voice, that Odeh and party colleagues skipped the first screening and "refused to take part in a moment of silence"; the moment-of-silence claim appears only in this right-leaning outlet and I found no corroboration. — [Srugim, 15 Nov 2023](https://www.srugim.co.il/864866-עודה-נגד-טבח-אוקטובר-לא-יכול-לקבל-את-זה)

**2024**
- 5 Mar 2024, University of Haifa conference (Hebrew): "כל הכיבוש הארור לא מצדיק כהוא זה פגיעה ביהודי אחד חף מפשע – אין מצב כזה"; "אני לא רוצה שהעם שלי יהרוג ילד או בן אדם או ייקח בשבי אישה בת 88, אסור בתכלית האיסור"; and "הטבח של השבעה באוקטובר מצדיק הרג של 35 אלף פלסטינים, 15 אלף ילדים מתחת לגיל 10? זה פשע נגד האנושות"; "ה-7.10 לא מצדיק פגיעה בילד פלסטיני בעזה". He uses the term "the massacre of 7 October". — [Radio Haifa 107.5, 5 Mar 2024](https://1075.fm/item/155440/)
- July 2024: removed from the plenum after calling Netanyahu "the biggest terrorist here". — [Times of Israel, 21 Jan 2025](https://www.timesofisrael.com/arab-mk-defends-celebrating-release-of-hostages-and-prisoners-after-backlash/)
- 10 Dec 2024, Knesset Science and Technology Committee (Hebrew). On 7 October: "בעיקר פגיעה מוסרית עמוקה מאוד" ("primarily a very deep moral injury"), referring to the abduction of a one-year-old and an 88-year-old woman and the killing of people at a party. Then: "אני מאמין שיש זכות לכל עם נכבש להיאבק בכובש, אני מאמין בזה. זה נכון בכל מקום בעולם" ("I believe every occupied people has the right to struggle against the occupier... this is true everywhere in the world"). Kikar's headline framed this as "Odeh on the 7 October massacre: 'the right to struggle against the occupier'"; Channel 14 used a similar headline (snippet only). Otzma Yehudit MKs petitioned for discipline over the remarks. — [Kikar HaShabbat, 10 Dec 2024](https://www.kikar.co.il/political-news/ayman-odeh-october-7-comments); [Times of Israel, 21 Jan 2025](https://www.timesofisrael.com/arab-mk-defends-celebrating-release-of-hostages-and-prisoners-after-backlash/)

**January 2025 post and clarification**
- 19 Jan 2025, X, as three hostages were released: "שמח על שחרור החטופים והאסירים" ("happy for the release of the hostages and prisoners"), continuing (English as reported) "Now we must free both peoples from the yoke of occupation. We were all born free." Otzma Yehudit MK Yitzhak Wasserlauf said he would gather signatures to oust him; unnamed right-wing and centrist parties called for his removal. — [Times of Israel, 21 Jan 2025](https://www.timesofisrael.com/arab-mk-defends-celebrating-release-of-hostages-and-prisoners-after-backlash/)
- 21 Jan 2025, clarification (no retraction): "I understand that many people were hurt by my remarks"; the hostage families "were also hurt, and that truly saddens me"; he was moved "as the abducted women, innocent of any crime, returned to their mothers' arms" and equally by the release of 90 Palestinian prisoners, whom he described as "minors, teenagers", most "not charged with anything at all"; "I am a citizen of Israel who also sees and feels the suffering of the Palestinian people." — [Times of Israel, 21 Jan 2025](https://www.timesofisrael.com/arab-mk-defends-celebrating-release-of-hostages-and-prisoners-after-backlash/)

**2025: "Gaza has won", sanctions, expulsion attempt**
- 31 May 2025, anti-war rally in Haifa: "Gaza has won, and Gaza will win"; "this is genocide, this is ethnic cleansing". Reactions: Tally Gotliv (Likud) called him a "terror supporter"; Oded Forer (Yisrael Beytenu) "pro-Israel or pro-Hamas"; minister Eli Cohen "a fifth column"; Haifa mayor Yona Yahav said such a person "isn't wanted in our city". Odeh did not retract; he wrote on X "annihilation is not victory", "Life is victory", "Yes, Gaza will win. Life will win", and said critics who see Gazans as "terrorists from birth" distorted his words. — [Times of Israel, 1 Jun 2025](https://www.timesofisrael.com/gaza-has-won-and-gaza-will-win-arab-mks-speech-at-anti-war-rally-draws-outrage/)
- 2 Jun 2025 (reported), interview with Saudi channel Al-Hadath (Arabic; Hebrew rendering by Maariv): "לא אכפת לי מי התחיל את המלחמה ואיך היא התחילה" ("I don't care who started the war or how it started"), in the context of civilian deaths and destroyed hospitals in Gaza. — [Maariv, 2 Jun 2025](https://www.maariv.co.il/news/politics/article-1201946)
- 23 Jun 2025: Ethics Committee (ruling on 49 complaints) suspended Odeh for two weeks without pay for saying the IDF was carrying out massacre and starvation; Touma-Sliman three days; Tibi a severe reprimand. — [Mako/N12, 23 Jun 2025](https://www.mako.co.il/news-politics/2025_q2/Article-81caa1d53fc9791027.htm)
- 24 Jun 2025, Knesset House Committee hearing on the expulsion request (initiated by Likud MK Avichai Boaron; 70 signatures gathered after a failed attempt in January): Odeh: "I do not retract, I do not apologize, and I will continue to struggle"; "You're trying to silence me because I always speak to both peoples." Hearing recessed without a vote. — [maki.org.il, June 2025](https://maki.org.il/en/?p=32789)
- 30 Jun 2025: House Committee approved the expulsion request, 14 of 17 members in favour (reported elsewhere as 14-2). The Knesset legal adviser's position, as quoted by Walla (snippet only), was that the 19 January post could not ground expulsion: "קשה לקרוא בה תמיכה חד משמעית בטרור" ("it is hard to read in it unequivocal support for terror"). — [Walla, 30 Jun 2025](https://news.walla.co.il/item/3764985); [Ground News aggregation](https://groundnews.lakegeneva.lib.wi.us/article/knesset-home-committee-votes-14-2-to-impeach-israeli-arab-mk-ayman-odeh)
- 14 Jul 2025, plenum: motion failed, 73 for, 15 against; 90 needed. United Torah Judaism and Blue and White boycotted; Lapid and most Yesh Atid MKs were absent; The Democrats voted against. Kan reports a first round of 68-15 before the 73-15 result, and lists Arab MKs, Labor MKs and Alon Schuster among those against (snippet only). After the vote Odeh posted: "The disgraceful and fascistic move against me failed." — [Times of Israel, 15 Jul 2025](https://www.timesofisrael.com/motion-to-expel-odeh-from-knesset-fails-to-pass-amid-heckling-by-coalition-members/); [Kan](https://www.kan.org.il/content/kan-news/politic/931147/); [Israel Hayom](https://www.israelhayom.co.il/news/politics/article/18414673)
- During the expulsion debate Odeh said he had condemned the massacre "a thousand times" ("אלף פעמים") (Shakuf, snippet only). — [Shakuf](https://shakuf.co.il/57573)
- Sept 2025, Mazeej podcast (Arabic; Hebrew rendering by Srugim): "הכיבוש הוא הטרוריסט הגדול ביותר" ("the occupation is the greatest terrorist"); on Gaza: "זו איננה תגובה ל־7 באוקטובר אלא שימוש באירוע כדי ליישם תוכניות ישנות" ("this is not a response to 7 October but use of the event to implement old plans"); "יש לי עמדה ברורה נגד הרג חפים מפשע". Srugim reports critics saw this as blurring the 7 October atrocities. — [Srugim, 11 Sep 2025](https://www.srugim.co.il/1245362-עודה-שוב-מעורר-סערה-הכיבוש-הוא-הטרוריס)
- 13 Oct 2025: Odeh and Cassif were removed from the plenum during President Trump's speech after holding "Recognize Palestine" signs. Before the speech Odeh criticized "the cynical use of October 7" and referred to "thousands of Israeli victims" and "the genocidal war". — [Jerusalem Post, 13 Oct 2025](https://www.jpost.com/israel-news/politics-and-diplomacy/article-870343)

**2026**
- 14 Feb 2026, Channel 12 "Meet the Press", asked repeatedly by Amit Segal "Is Hamas a terror organization or not?": "I oppose Hamas politically and ideologically. I have condemned October 7th a thousand times."; "I condemned all harm to civilians since the 1990s, and until now."; "I know who gave Hamas weapons already in the late 1980s." He did not say yes or no. Segal's characterization: Odeh avoided the label for "the second consecutive interview and the hundredth since October 7" and had not condemned the attacks on IDF bases or the killing of soldiers. Reported by a right-wing outlet; English rendering of a Hebrew broadcast. — [Arutz Sheva / Israel National News, 14 Feb 2026](https://www.israelnationalnews.com/news/422404)
- May 2026: replaced as Hadash chair by Yousef Jabareen; did not run for the 26th Knesset (he had announced in May 2023 that he would not run again). — [Wikipedia, citing Walla 16 May 2026](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ayman_Odeh)

### Inferences
- Constant across three years: condemnation of killing/abducting civilians, stated in Hebrew and (per Hebrew renderings) in Arabic-language media. I found no statement in which he denied or doubted specific atrocities.
- Not found in any quote: a statement that Hamas is a terrorist organization; a condemnation of the 7 October attacks on military bases/soldiers. The second is Segal's claim; I found nothing that rebuts or confirms it beyond the absence of such a quote.
- Statements critics read as contextualization or justification: "right of every occupied people to struggle against the occupier" (10 Dec 2024, same remarks in which he called 7 October a moral injury), "Gaza has won" (31 May 2025), "I don't care who started the war" (June 2025), "the occupation is the greatest terrorist" (Sept 2025).
- No outright self-contradiction found. The nearest tension is between "nothing, not even the occupation, justifies harming innocents" (Oct 2023, Mar 2024) and "every occupied people has the right to struggle against the occupier" (Dec 2024); he presented them together as compatible (civilians excluded).

### Gaps
- Odeh's own post or statement from 7 October 2023 itself: no statement found.
- The Hebrew full text of the 19 Jan 2025 post beyond the opening phrase was not retrieved (Israel Hayom and Kan pages returned 403).
- Outcome of the Otzma Yehudit ethics petition over the 10 Dec 2024 remarks: not found.
- Anniversary statements (7 Oct 2024, 2025, 2026): no statement found.
- A 2024 post-viewing Hebrew-language interview pressed on "is Hamas a terror organization": none found before Feb 2026.

## 3. Ahmad Tibi (Ta'al chair; No. 2 on the Joint List in 2026)

### Takeaway
Tibi condemned the killing of civilians from the Knesset podium in October 2023 ("murder of women, children and the elderly and atrocities against civilians in the south deserve denunciation without hesitation") and has repeated that position since, while consistently declining, in 2023 and again in October-November 2026, to call Hamas a terrorist organization.

### Cited Findings
- 7 Oct 2023: per The Seventh Eye, Tibi answered Amit Segal's complaint by stating a principled position "against harm to civilians whoever they are", and said he had acted since the morning to prevent friction and incitement (described, not quoted verbatim). — [The Seventh Eye, 26 Dec 2023](https://www.the7eye.org.il/505126)
- 11 Oct 2023 (reported), interview with an Arabic-language channel (Hebrew rendering by Arutz Sheva, which names it "Al-Hadath"): the Arab leadership opposes the war "מבחינה עקרונית ואנושית"; on Hamas leaders' call to Arab citizens to join: "לנו יש ייחודיות כתושבים שגרים בארץ, והייחודיות הזו מחייבת אותנו בנוגע לדרך בה אנו נאבקים"; "אנחנו רחוקים מפעילות צבאית"; women and children taken to Gaza should be returned "בתמורה לשחרור נשים וקטינים המוחזקים בבתי הסוהר בישראל" ("in exchange for the release of women and minors held in Israeli prisons"). The outlet notes he avoided condemning Hamas as a terror organization. — [Arutz Sheva (Hebrew), 11 Oct 2023](https://www.inn.co.il/news/616442)
- October 2023, Knesset plenum (Maariv: "days" before 2 Nov; likely 12 or 16 Oct): "רצח נשים וילדים וקשישים וזוועות נגד אזרחים שהיו בדרום ראויים להוקעה ללא היסוס"; "למוסר האנושי אין סלקטיביות, אין חצי מוסר. רצח ילדים הוא רצח ילדים" ("Human morality has no selectivity, no half-morality. The murder of children is the murder of children"). — [Maariv, 2 Nov 2023](https://www.maariv.co.il/news/politics/article-1049374); [Local Call, 15 Oct 2023](https://www.mekomit.co.il/מאז-המשטר-הצבאי-הערבים-בישראל-לא-פחדו/)
- 2 Nov 2023, Knesset Health Committee: MK Pnina Tamano-Shata (National Unity) accused Tibi and colleagues of skipping the screening of the atrocity footage ("בחרתם לא להסתכל על הזוועות"); Tibi demanded a retraction; she replied "אתנצל רק אם תגנה את חמאס, ותודה שהוא ארגון טרור" ("I'll apologize only if you condemn Hamas and admit it is a terror organization"). The report does not record Tibi doing so. — [Maariv, 2 Nov 2023](https://www.maariv.co.il/news/politics/article-1049374)
- 15 Nov 2023: journalist Avishai Grinzaig (then i24/Globes) posted that Tibi said in 2018 "חמאס הוא לא ארגון טרור... פלג לגיטימי שרבים בעם הפלסטיני בוחרים בו בקלפיות", and that after 7 October he asked Tibi's spokeswoman and Tibi directly whether he had changed his position and was ignored. This is the journalist's account. — [Avishai Grinzaig on X, 15 Nov 2023](https://x.com/avishaigrinzaig/status/1724751889227469220)
- Hebrew Wikipedia records that Tibi said in 2009 and again in 2019 that he does not regard Hamas as a terror organization, and that in 2023 he condemned Hamas following the massacre (citing the Local Call quote above). — [Hebrew Wikipedia, "אחמד טיבי"](https://he.wikipedia.org/wiki/אחמד_טיבי)

### Inferences
- The faction's common formula across 2023-2026: condemn harm to (or the "massacre"/"slaughter" of) civilians; attribute the root cause to the occupation; decline the "terrorist organization" label for Hamas. Cassif goes furthest in naming Hamas as perpetrator; Touma-Sliman has the thinnest direct record.
- Mainstream and left-leaning sources (The Seventh Eye, Local Call, AP) describe the faction as having condemned the attack; right-leaning sources (Arutz Sheva, Srugim, Channel 14, JNS, Makor Rishon) emphasize the refusal to name Hamas a terror organization, the skipped screening and the contextualizing statements. The underlying quotes largely overlap; the difference is in selection and framing.
