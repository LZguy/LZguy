# The claim "Arab MKs did not condemn 7 October" and the formal Knesset record (7 Oct 2023 - 18 Nov 2026)

Researcher note on method and limits: ~40 search/fetch calls, Hebrew and English. Several pages returned HTTP 403 (Makor Rishon, Israel Hayom, Shakuf, Channel 14) and are cited only from search-result summaries; these are flagged "(search summary only)". Search summaries are less reliable than full-page reads. Anything labelled UNVERIFIED was not confirmed on a fetched primary page. Per-MK statements are covered by other researchers and appear here only where they are the object of a formal proceeding.

## 1. Who has asserted that Arab MKs did not condemn (or supported) the 7 October attack?

### Takeaway
The generalized claim is documented from the day of the attack itself (Amit Segal, 7 Oct 2023; Channel 14 presenters, 15-16 Oct 2023) and by 2025-2026 mostly appears in the broader form "terror supporters" (Netanyahu, Ofir Katz, Likud, bereaved-families letter) rather than the narrow form "did not condemn". I found few verbatim instances of the narrow claim by named ministers; that is a gap, not evidence of absence.

### Cited Findings
- 7 Oct 2023: Channel 12 / Yedioth journalist Amit Segal complained on the day of the attack, before Shabbat ended, that MKs Ahmad Tibi and Ayman Odeh had not condemned the Hamas massacre; he deleted the post after Tibi responded. Only a paraphrase of Segal's words is available, not the verbatim text. — [The Seventh Eye, Oren Persico, 26.12.2023](https://www.the7eye.org.il/505126)
- Tibi's reply to Segal (paraphrase): their principled position opposes harm to civilians and he had been working since the morning to prevent friction and incitement. — [The Seventh Eye](https://www.the7eye.org.il/505126)
- 15 Oct 2023: Channel 14's Shai Golden said that while Mansour Abbas issued a condemnation, "Ahmad Tibi and the rest of the gang" were "ממלאה פיה מים" (keeping silent). — [The Seventh Eye](https://www.the7eye.org.il/505126)
- 16 Oct 2023: a Channel 14 presenter (unnamed in the summary) said Arab public figures, apart from Abbas who condemned "half-heartedly", were all silent and no one condemned "in the strongest terms". — [The Seventh Eye](https://www.the7eye.org.il/505126)
- Feb 2024 (Cassif impeachment): Likud's statement attacked Lapid for "saving" a "terror supporter"; coalition whip Ofir Katz called Cassif "a supporter of terrorism"; Oded Forer: "Someone who supports the armed struggle against the State of Israel and decides that Israel is carrying out genocide can't be a member of Knesset." — [Times of Israel](https://www.timesofisrael.com/bid-to-oust-mk-ofer-cassif-over-backing-for-icj-genocide-case-falls-short-in-knesset/)
- 30 June 2025: House Committee chair Ofir Katz on Odeh: "הוא אמור להירקב בכלא" ("he should rot in prison") (headline quote). — [Israel Hayom](https://www.israelhayom.co.il/news/politics/article/18310509) (search summary only); [Maariv](https://www.maariv.co.il/news/law/article-1210157) (search summary only)
- 14 July 2025 (Odeh plenum debate): Katz on Odeh: "He's not a terror supporter, he's a terrorist." — [Times of Israel](https://www.timesofisrael.com/motion-to-expel-odeh-from-knesset-fails-to-pass-amid-heckling-by-coalition-members/)
- Sept 2026: Likud's petition to the Central Elections Committee alleged the Joint List "supports Hamas's armed struggle against Israel"; Otzma Yehudit's petition against Sami Abu Shehadeh centred on an article he published on 8 Oct 2023. — [Davar, 23.09.2026](https://www.davar1.co.il/698227/)
- 10 Nov 2026 (swearing-in of the 26th Knesset): PM Netanyahu called the Arab parties "תומכי טרור שונאי ישראל" ("terror supporters who hate Israel") and said the difference between Ra'am and the Joint List is only "which state will be established" in place of Israel. Likud said he "עומד מאחורי דבריו". Yair Golan accused him of incitement against the Arab public; Mansour Abbas replied that Ra'am does not seek an Islamist or Arab state and supports two states. Joint List MKs left the plenum before the anthem. — [Haaretz live blog, 10.11.2026](https://www.haaretz.co.il/news/elections/2026-11-10/ty-article-live/000001a1-c52e-d9c8-a5f9-ed6e1b540000)
- 15 Nov 2026: about 200 bereaved families / families of terror victims wrote to MK Matan Kahana urging him not to be elected Knesset Speaker with Arab-party votes. Quotes from the letter: the Arab parties "מסרבות להכיר בחמאס ובחיזבאללה כארגוני טרור"; "כשאנחנו קברנו את מתינו, הם לא הזילו דמעה"; members "אף מזדהים עם מעשיהם". The article carried no response from the Arab parties. — [Walla, 15.11.2026](https://www.walla.co.il/news/politics/383961538)
- Opinion pieces making a softer version of the claim: a Maariv op-ed says not enough explicit condemnations of Hamas were heard. — [Maariv opinion](https://www.maariv.co.il/news/opinions/article-1388803) (search summary only; date not captured)
- A Makor Rishon profile of the 19 MKs elected from Arab lists (2026) is headlined "קידום חרמות על ישראל והטלת ספק בטבח" ("promoting boycotts of Israel and casting doubt on the massacre"); the same piece reportedly states that Abbas "condemned the 7 October massacre unequivocally". — [Makor Rishon](https://www.makorrishon.co.il/news/politcal/article/389924) (search summary only; page returned 403; right-leaning outlet)

### Inferences
- The narrow factual claim ("did not condemn") is best documented in the first days after the attack; later rhetoric by senior politicians that I could document uses the wider label "terror supporters", which is not checkable in the same way.
- The bereaved-families letter (Nov 2026) shifts the test from "condemned the massacre" to "recognise Hamas as a terror organisation" and to emotional identification ("did not shed a tear").

### Gaps
- No verbatim text of Segal's deleted 7 Oct 2023 post.
- I did not find verbatim, dated instances of the exact sentence "the Arab MKs did not condemn the massacre" from named ministers (Ben Gvir, Karhi, Gotliv, Liberman etc.), though searches were run in Hebrew. Likud 2026 campaign material was not located directly.
- Channel 14 presenter of 16 Oct 2023 is unnamed in my source.

## 2. How did fact-checkers, journalists and research institutes assess the claim?

### Takeaway
I found no dedicated verdict by Globes' "המשרוקית", Fake Reporter, Bodkim, AFP or Reuters on this specific claim. The main documented assessment is The Seventh Eye / Representation Index study (Dec 2023), which lists dated condemnations by Abbas, Hadash/Maki, Cassif and Odeh and concludes mainstream media did not report most of them in real time.

### Cited Findings
- The Seventh Eye (media-watch outlet; "מדד הייצוג" project with Sikkuy-Aufoq and Yifat Media Research), 26.12.2023, headline: "הפוליטיקאים הערבים גינו את הטבח שערך חמאס, התקשורת הישראלית התעלמה". It reports: Abbas was mentioned several times as having condemned; condemnations by Odeh and by Hadash-Ta'al were not covered by mainstream media; Yifat found no mainstream real-time coverage of those statements. — [The Seventh Eye](https://www.the7eye.org.il/505126)
- Statements the study lists as condemnations (wording as given there):
  - 7 Oct 2023, Mansour Abbas: referred to "האירועים המצערים, הטרגיים והמגונים".
  - 7 Oct 2023, Hadash and Maki statement: "repeatedly express an unequivocal condemnation of any harm to innocent civilians".
  - 7 Oct 2023, Ofer Cassif: nothing justifies "crimes and massacre" as carried out that day in the south.
  - 10 Oct 2023, Odeh: nothing justifies harming innocent civilians; condemned the Hamas leadership's call on Arab citizens to join the fight.
  - 11 Oct 2023, Odeh: nothing justifies harming innocents, "neither in the south nor in Gaza".
  - 12 Oct 2023, Odeh in the Knesset: killing of women, children and the elderly in the south deserves condemnation "without hesitation".
  - 26 Oct 2023, High Follow-Up Committee press conference (Mohammad Barakeh): condemned harm to innocents, Jews and Palestinians alike.
  — [The Seventh Eye](https://www.the7eye.org.il/505126)
- Context numbers from the same project: in 2023 Arabs were 2.8% of current-affairs speakers on mainstream broadcast TV (about 18% of the population); Channel 14 0.7% in its two most popular programmes (April 2024). These figures come from related Representation Index items listed on the page, not from the article body. — [The Seventh Eye](https://www.the7eye.org.il/505126)
- Ethics Committee's own record on Iman Khatib-Yasin (see section 3) notes that in the same 5.11.23 interview in which she denied babies were slaughtered and women raped, she also called the 7 October massacre "לא אנושי", contrary to Islam, and "לא הדרך לפתור סכסוך מתמשך"; and that her written apology that evening said she "גינתה ועודנה מגנה את הפשעים שביצעו החמאס באזרחי ישראל". — [Ethics Committee decision 18/25, 15.11.2023, hosted by Adalah](https://www.adalah.org/uploads/uploads/Khatib-Yassin_Suspension_15_Nov_2023.pdf)
- Opinion/analysis (not fact-check): Dr. Rafi Nets-Zehngut, Zman Yisrael, 13.11.2026: "חברי כנסת ערבים מרכזיים מכול המפלגות הערביות גינו טרור ככלל ואת טבח ה-7 באוקטובר בפרט." No MKs named or sources cited in the piece. — [Zman Yisrael](https://www.zman.co.il/740831/)
- Shakuf (independent, reader-funded outlet) wrote that the Arab MKs do not "support" terror and "גינו ומגנים אותו", in a piece noting Netanyahu voted 771 times and Ben Gvir 802 times together with Arab MKs. — [Shakuf](https://shakuf.co.il/65631) (search summary only; page 403)
- Cassif, Sept 2026, after his disqualification by the Central Elections Committee: said his condemnation of the Gaza war does not contradict his condemnation of the 7 October massacre and Hamas's war crimes. — [Davar](https://www.davar1.co.il/698227/)

### Inferences
- The available assessments agree that condemnations by leading Arab-party MKs exist in the record from 7-12 Oct 2023; the disputes concern their wording (many condemn "harm to civilians on both sides" rather than Hamas by name), their timing, and separate statements by individual MKs (Khatib-Yasin's denial; Abu Shehadeh's 8 Oct 2023 article) that critics cite.
- The principal sources asserting that condemnations occurred (Seventh Eye, Shakuf, Zman op-ed) are media-critical or left-leaning; no right-leaning fact-check contesting the listed quotes was found.

### Gaps
- No Globes "המשרוקית" check on this exact claim was found (two Hebrew searches). The Whistle's index page is [here](https://www.globes.co.il/news/home.aspx?fid=10559) for manual checking. Likewise nothing from Fake Reporter, Bodkim, AFP or Reuters fact-check.
- The Seventh Eye quotes are partly paraphrased in my extraction; original posts/Knesset transcript not retrieved.

## 3. Knesset Ethics Committee decisions, Oct 2023 - Nov 2026

### Takeaway
At least seven sanctions against Arab-party MKs/Cassif for war-related statements are documented (2023-2025), ranging from a severe reprimand to a six-month removal; only one (Khatib-Yasin) concerned the facts of 7 October itself, the others concerned accusations against the IDF/Israel. Jewish MKs sanctioned in the same period received reprimands, remarks or a speaking ban. By 23 June 2025 the committee had decided 49 war-related complaints and sent 28 rejection letters.

### Cited Findings
Arab-party MKs and Cassif:
- 18 Oct 2023 — Ofer Cassif (Hadash-Ta'al): removed for 45 days, 14 days' pay docked. Statements: a remark two days after the attack that "Israel wanted this violence", and an interview comparing an Israeli war plan to the Nazis' Final Solution. About 400 complaints. Cassif: "another nail in the coffin of freedom of political expression". — [Jerusalem Post](https://www.jpost.com/israel-news/politics-and-diplomacy/article-768978); [Haaretz English, 19.10.2023](https://www.haaretz.com/israel-news/2023-10-19/ty-article/.premium/knesset-ethics-committee-suspends-docks-pay-of-mk-for-tying-gaza-war-to-holocaust/0000018b-4815-d614-abcf-e87795490000) (search summary)
- 18 Oct 2023 — the committee adopted a principled "zero tolerance" decision on wartime statements "שעלולות לפגוע בהתמודדויות של מדינת ישראל ושל הציבור בימי הלחימה; שמבזות או מכפישות את אזרחי ישראל". — [Decision 18/25](https://www.adalah.org/uploads/uploads/Khatib-Yassin_Suspension_15_Nov_2023.pdf)
- 15 Nov 2023 — Iman Khatib-Yasin (Ra'am), Decision 18/25: removal from plenum and committees for one month (20.11.23-20.12.23), two weeks' pay; may enter to vote only. Statement (Knesset Channel, 5.11.23): "לא שחטו תינוקות, לפחות ממה שהיה בסרט, ולא אנסו נשים". Complainants: MKs Malinovsky, Liberman, Forer, Sova, Amar, Son Har-Melech, Kroizer, Vaturi and citizens. She apologised in writing that evening, before the committee on 7.11.23, and from the plenum podium on 13.11.23 ("ב-7 באוקטובר בוצעו מעשים דחויים ופסולים ערכית, אנושית ודתית, שאין להם שום הצדקה"). The committee found the interview amounted to "הכחשה של הטבח וההתעללות", decided unanimously, noted MKs had begun collecting signatures for her impeachment and held that the ethical/parliamentary track was the appropriate one. Chair: MK Yinon Azoulay (Shas). — [Decision 18/25 (primary document)](https://www.adalah.org/uploads/uploads/Khatib-Yassin_Suspension_15_Nov_2023.pdf)
- Ra'am's leadership said after the interview that she had no place on its list and demanded she resign immediately. — [Maariv](https://www.maariv.co.il/news/politics/article-1050671) (search summary). She did not resign and was re-elected in 2026. — [Davar, 10.11.2026](https://www.davar1.co.il/704821/)
- 15 Nov 2023 — Aida Touma-Sliman (Hadash-Ta'al): removal for two months, two weeks' pay; may vote. Statement: wrote that "צה״ל תוקף בפצצות זרחן את בית החולים שיפא". — [Haaretz, 15.11.2023](https://www.haaretz.co.il/news/politi/2023-11-15/ty-article/0000018b-d270-dffa-adef-f670f1f90000)
- Nov 2024 (a Monday; UNVERIFIED exact date, reported as 11 Nov 2024; in force until 12 May 2025) — Ofer Cassif: removal from plenum and committees for six months, two weeks' pay. Grounds: statements accusing the IDF and Israel of war crimes, and signing the petition supporting South Africa's ICJ case; committee wording: "הפרת כללי האתיקה באופן בוטה וקשה", "עידוד עלילות דם נגד חיילי צה"ל". 25 complaints. Unanimous. Chair: Moshe Roth (UTJ); members named: Michal Woldiger (Religious Zionism), Pnina Tamano-Shata (National Unity), Sharon Nir (Yisrael Beiteinu). Cassif: "political-nationalist censorship". — [Ynet](https://www.ynet.co.il/news/article/bkuppnyfjg); [News24](https://www.news24.com/world/israels-parliament-bans-and-gags-lawmaker-ofer-cassif-for-supporting-sas-genocide-case-at-icj-20241112) (search summary)
- By 23 June 2025 — Ayman Odeh: removed for two weeks without pay, for saying the IDF/Israel is carrying out "טבח והרעבה" in Gaza. — [N12, 23.06.2025](https://www.mako.co.il/news-politics/2025_q2/Article-81caa1d53fc9791027.htm)
- By 23 June 2025 — Aida Touma-Sliman: removed for three days, for accusing the IDF of a massacre / war crimes. — [N12](https://www.mako.co.il/news-politics/2025_q2/Article-81caa1d53fc9791027.htm)
- By 23 June 2025 — Ahmad Tibi: severe reprimand for calling IDF soldiers "רוצחים במדים". N12 gives the statement date as "21.8.23", which precedes the war and may be a typo; UNVERIFIED. — [N12](https://www.mako.co.il/news-politics/2025_q2/Article-81caa1d53fc9791027.htm)
- 2025 (date UNVERIFIED, apparently the same June 2025 batch) — Ofer Cassif: removed for two months with two weeks' pay cut, for "a systematic, consistent pattern" of statements accusing IDF soldiers of war crimes; described as his second/third suspension. — [Maki](https://maki.org.il/%D7%95%D7%A2%D7%93%D7%AA-%D7%94%D7%90%D7%AA%D7%99%D7%A7%D7%94-%D7%94%D7%97%D7%9C%D7%99%D7%98%D7%94-%D7%9C%D7%94%D7%A9%D7%A2%D7%95%D7%AA-%D7%9E%D7%94%D7%9B%D7%A0%D7%A1%D7%AA-%D7%90%D7%AA-%D7%97%D7%9B/) (party site, partisan; search summary); [i24NEWS](https://www.i24news.tv/he/news/news/politics/artc-65624102) (search summary)

Jewish MKs, same period (all from the N12 round-up of 23.06.2025):
- Nissim Vaturi (Likud): reprimand for five statements, incl. "יש כמה זרועות לחמאס - זרוע הלחימה של המחבלים הארורים שרוצחת ילדים ואת הזרוע של ההפגנות".
- Itamar Ben Gvir, Tally Gotliv, Nissim Vaturi, May Golan, Ofir Katz: reprimand each for calling other MKs, mainly Cassif and Tibi, "מחבלים" (terrorists).
- May Golan: barred from speaking for four plenum sittings (confrontation with MK Merav Ben Ari).
- Michal Shir (Yesh Atid): a remark (הערה) for calling Netanyahu "סייען חמאס".
- Naama Lazimi (Labor/Democrats): reprimand for a sign "אתה הראש, אתה אשם" and related conduct.
- Hanoch Milwidsky (Likud), as deputy speaker: reprimand for offensive terms toward Cassif and improper removals.
- Vladimir Beliak (Yesh Atid): reprimand for comparing Otzma Yehudit to a neo-Nazi party, calling Simcha Rothman "אחד שאחראי לטבח 7 באוקטובר", and mocking Almog Cohen's PTSD.
— [N12, 23.06.2025](https://www.mako.co.il/news-politics/2025_q2/Article-81caa1d53fc9791027.htm)
- Totals as of 23.06.2025: 49 complaints decided concerning war-period statements; 28 rejection letters. Another outlet phrases it as 10 decisions on 49 complaints. — [N12](https://www.mako.co.il/news-politics/2025_q2/Article-81caa1d53fc9791027.htm); [i24NEWS](https://www.i24news.tv/he/news/news/politics/artc-65624102) (search summary)

### Inferences
- In the documented decisions, removal from the Knesset (days to months) was applied only to Arab-party MKs and Cassif; Jewish MKs received reprimands, a remark, or a four-sitting speaking ban. This is a description of the listed cases, not a complete census.
- Only the Khatib-Yasin decision concerns denial of 7 October facts; the committee itself recorded her simultaneous and subsequent condemnations.

### Gaps
- No Ethics Committee decisions from July 2025 - Nov 2026 were found (e.g. whether Odeh and Cassif were sanctioned after being ejected during President Trump's Knesset speech on 13 Oct 2025; the ejection itself is reported [here](https://www.fox5dc.com/news/knesset-members-removed-during-trumps-speech-israeli-parliament)).
- Exact dates of the June 2025 decisions and of Cassif's two-month removal not confirmed on the Knesset site (main.knesset.gov.il not retrieved).
- Other Jewish-MK cases (e.g. statements about Gaza such as "nuclear bomb"/"erase Gaza") were not checked; Amichai Eliyahu's remark was made as a minister and its ethics handling is unknown to me.

## 4. Impeachment proceedings under Basic Law: The Knesset s.42A(c)

### Takeaway
Both attempts failed in the plenum: Cassif 85 in favour (19 Feb 2024), Odeh 73-15 (14 July 2025); 90 are required. Both passed the House Committee 14-2. In both, centrist opposition parties gave freedom of vote and largely absented themselves; in the Odeh vote UTJ also boycotted.

### Cited Findings
Ofer Cassif (Jan-Feb 2024):
- Trigger: Cassif signed a petition backing South Africa's genocide case against Israel at the ICJ (Jan 2024). Initiator: Oded Forer (Yisrael Beiteinu), first use of the 2016 "Suspension Law" mechanism. 85 MKs signed the request (70 needed). — [Times of Israel](https://www.timesofisrael.com/bid-to-oust-mk-ofer-cassif-over-backing-for-icj-genocide-case-falls-short-in-knesset/); [JNS](https://www.jns.org/israel-news/knesset-advances-expulsion-of-mk-who-supported-icj-genocide-case)
- House Committee (chair Ofir Katz, Likud): approved 14-2 after two days of debate (13 of 17 needed). — [JNS](https://www.jns.org/israel-news/knesset-advances-expulsion-of-mk-who-supported-icj-genocide-case) (search summary); [Jerusalem Post](https://www.jpost.com/breaking-news/article-784343)
- Legal position: Deputy Attorney General Avital Sompolinsky and Knesset legal adviser Sagit Afik said the allegations did not cross the criminal threshold required for removal. — [Times of Israel](https://www.timesofisrael.com/bid-to-oust-mk-ofer-cassif-over-backing-for-icj-genocide-case-falls-short-in-knesset/)
- Plenum, 19 Feb 2024: 85 in favour, five short of 90. Number against not captured (UNVERIFIED; commonly reported as 11). — [Times of Israel](https://www.timesofisrael.com/bid-to-oust-mk-ofer-cassif-over-backing-for-icj-genocide-case-falls-short-in-knesset/); [Ynetnews](https://www.ynetnews.com/article/s1h3um113t)
- Who voted how: Yisrael Beiteinu unanimously in favour; nearly all of Likud, Religious Zionism, Otzma Yehudit in favour; Netanyahu backed it. Yesh Atid and National Unity gave freedom of vote: most Yesh Atid MKs did not take part, Lapid absent and said he would not vote for it ("he would return here a hero" after a High Court reversal); National Unity split, Gantz absent. All Labor MKs boycotted. Hadash-Ta'al and Ra'am opposed (Odeh and Khatib-Yasin spoke in his defence). Shas/UTJ votes not specified in my sources. — [Times of Israel](https://www.timesofisrael.com/bid-to-oust-mk-ofer-cassif-over-backing-for-icj-genocide-case-falls-short-in-knesset/); [Jerusalem Post](https://www.jpost.com/israel-news/politics-and-diplomacy/article-787917) (search summary)
- Cassif: "Only in an Orwellian world where truth is a lie" can his signature be presented as support for violence. — [Times of Israel](https://www.timesofisrael.com/bid-to-oust-mk-ofer-cassif-over-backing-for-icj-genocide-case-falls-short-in-knesset/)

Ayman Odeh (Jan / June-July 2025):
- Trigger: an X post in January 2025 after the hostage deal, saying he was "happy for the release of hostages and [Palestinian] prisoners". Initiator: Avichai Boaron (Likud); a January signature drive fell short; resubmitted in June with 70 signatures including at least 10 from the opposition. Additional statement cited: a speech at an anti-war protest in Haifa, "Gaza has won and Gaza will win" (Times of Israel dates it to May; Jerusalem Post says a June rally — sources conflict). — [Times of Israel](https://www.timesofisrael.com/motion-to-expel-odeh-from-knesset-fails-to-pass-amid-heckling-by-coalition-members/); [Jerusalem Post via Yahoo](https://www.yahoo.com/news/knesset-vote-oust-mk-ayman-173854353.html)
- Knesset legal advice: the petition covered only the one X post; other statements could not be considered because signatures were not attached to them as required; the legal team advised against recycling the January petition. — [Jerusalem Post via Yahoo](https://www.yahoo.com/news/knesset-vote-oust-mk-ayman-173854353.html); [Haaretz, 30.06.2025](https://www.haaretz.co.il/news/politi/2025-06-30/ty-article/.premium/00000197-bd0b-df21-a1df-fdcb96130000) (search summary)
- House Committee: hearings 23 and 30 June 2025; vote 30 June 2025: 14-2. In favour included Yesh Atid and National Unity representatives (Pnina Tamano-Shata named). Against: Ahmad Tibi (Hadash-Ta'al) and Walid Taha (Ra'am). — [Haaretz](https://www.haaretz.co.il/news/politi/2025-06-30/ty-article/.premium/00000197-bd0b-df21-a1df-fdcb96130000) (search summary); [Walla](https://www.walla.co.il/news/breaking-news/3761497) (search summary)
- Plenum, 14 July 2025: 73 in favour, 15 against, 32 not voting; failed. In favour: coalition (other than UTJ) and Yisrael Beiteinu, which urged "all Zionist factions" to back it; Shas reportedly sided with the coalition after threatening a boycott. Against: Arab parties and The Democrats; "several" Yesh Atid and Blue and White MKs reported as voting against (per New Arab citing Haaretz) while another account says "a number of" Yesh Atid MKs supported — sources conflict. Boycotted: UTJ (over the draft bill) and Gantz's Blue and White-National Unity. ## 7. Survey data on Arab Israeli attitudes to 7 October

### Takeaway
The most-cited figure is the IDI's Nov-Dec 2023 finding that 56% of Arab Israelis agreed that the attack "does not reflect Arab society, the Palestinian people and Islamic values" (33% disagreed, 11% don't know). Other polls use different questions and are not directly comparable.

### Cited Findings
- IDI (fieldwork 27 Nov - 4 Dec 2023; published 26.12.2023; n=538, phone, Arabic; ±4.31%): 56% agree with Mansour Abbas's statement that the 7 October attack does not reflect Arab society and Islamic values. By religion: Druze 69.5%, Christians 68%, Muslims 53%. By 2022 vote: Zionist parties 76%, Ra'am 66%, Hadash-Ta'al 65%, Balad 39%. Men 61.5%, women 50.5%. — [IDI](https://en.idi.org.il/articles/52016)
- Same survey: 33% disagreed with the statement and 11% "didn't know". — [JNS](https://www.jns.org/israel-news/a-third-of-arab-israelis-see-oct-7-massacre-as-in-tune-with-islamic-values) (search summary; JNS headline frames this as "a third see Oct. 7 massacre as in tune with Islamic values" — the outlet's characterisation)
- Same survey: 65% feel part of Israel and its problems (70% in a 5-6 Nov 2023 survey); 86.5% support civilian volunteering during the war; 71% do not feel comfortable expressing themselves on social media; 84% worried about physical security. The survey did not ask directly about support for the attack. — [IDI](https://en.idi.org.il/articles/52016)
- Konrad Adenauer Program / Moshe Dayan Center (Tel Aviv University), late 2023: 47% of the Arab public justified Israel's response to the Hamas attack, 44% did not; about half thought the Hamas attack does not help solve the Palestinian problem. — [Maariv](https://www.maariv.co.il/news/politics/article-1056837) (search summary); [Ice](https://www.ice.co.il/research/news/article/990922) (search summary)
- Same program, June 2024 in-depth survey: 69.1% see no horizon for ending the conflict; roughly 30-34% support Israel's war aim of toppling Hamas (wording unclear in summary). — [Dayan Center](https://dayan.org/content/depth-survey-arab-public-shadow-ongoing-war-between-israel-and-hamas) (search summary)
- JPPI, March-April 2024 (n=613): 4% of Arab Israelis support continued Hamas rule in Gaza. — [JPPI](https://jppi.org.il/en/%D7%9E%D7%93%D7%93-%D7%94%D7%97%D7%91%D7%A8%D7%94-%D7%94%D7%99%D7%A9%D7%A8%D7%90%D7%9C%D7%99%D7%AA-%D7%9C%D7%97%D7%95%D7%93%D7%A9-%D7%90%D7%A4%D7%A8%D7%99%D7%9C-%D7%A8%D7%A7-4-%D7%9E%D7%A2%D7%A8/) (search summary)
- Palestinian-Israeli Pulse joint poll, Sept 2024: among Arab Israelis, 50% said 7 October was not justified by the blockade on Gaza, 27% said it was, 22% didn't know. — [PCPSR summary report](https://www.pcpsr.org/sites/default/files/Summary%20Report_%20English_Joint%20Poll%2012%20Sept%202024.pdf) (search summary)
- An early post-attack poll cited in a Jerusalem Post op-ed: 77% of Arab citizens condemned the attack and 85% opposed kidnapping civilians. Original pollster not identified in my source (possibly aChord/Agam) — UNVERIFIED. — [Jerusalem Post opinion](https://www.jpost.com/opinion/article-914687) (search summary)
- Post-election 2026 poll by Yousef Makladeh: 43% of Arab citizens agree Hamas should be recognised as a terrorist organisation, 30% disagree, 26% don't know. — source appeared only in a search summary without a clear URL attribution (likely the same [Jerusalem Post opinion](https://www.jpost.com/opinion/article-914687)); UNVERIFIED.
- Caution: the 67% "legitimate resistance" figure (Arab Center, 16 countries) and the 71% PCPSR figure refer to the Arab world / Palestinians in the West Bank and Gaza, not Arab citizens of Israel. — [Jerusalem Post](https://www.jpost.com/arab-israeli-conflict/article-782004); [Arutz 7](https://www.inn.co.il/news/632657)

## 8. 2025-2026 developments: denial law, elections, disqualification hearings

### Takeaway
The Knesset criminalised denial of the 7 October massacre on 21 Jan 2025 (16-0). In Sept 2026 the Central Elections Committee disqualified Ra'am, the Joint List, Cassif and Balad chair Abu Shehadeh; the Supreme Court reversed all but Abu Shehadeh, who withdrew after the court signalled a majority for his disqualification over his 8 Oct 2023 article. Elections were held on 27 Oct 2026; the Joint List won 12 seats and Ra'am 7.

### Cited Findings
- 21 Jan 2025: law criminalising denial, minimisation or celebration of the 7 October massacre, up to five years' imprisonment; sponsor Oded Forer (Yisrael Beiteinu); modelled on the 1986 Holocaust-denial law; applies to statements made with intent to defend, express sympathy for or identify with Hamas and its partners; third reading 16-0. ACRI's Gil Gan-Mor warned of a chilling effect on speech. — [Times of Israel](https://timesofisrael.com/knesset-passes-law-banning-denial-of-october-7-massacre); [Jerusalem Post](https://www.jpost.com/breaking-news/article-838603)
- 23 Sept 2026, Central Elections Committee (chair Justice Noam Solberg):
  - Sami Abu Shehadeh (Balad chair), petition by Otzma Yehudit, centred on an article of 8 Oct 2023 (per Davar: argued that Gaza, "small and besieged", had managed to keep Israeli intelligence out, so others could too). Disqualified 31-4 (support for armed struggle) and 30-4 (denial of Jewish and democratic state). Solberg voted in favour — reported as the first time in decades a chair backed a disqualification. AG Baharav-Miara said there was a basis for disqualifying him. Abu Shehadeh: "כשכתבתי את המאמר לא ידעתי מה גודל הזוועות" and that he would not have written it that way.
  - Ra'am, petitions by Otzma Yehudit, Likud and the "Choosing Life" forum (alleged links to associations tied to terror organisations, incl. "Aid 48"): disqualified 18-5, 1 abstention; against the AG's position according to Abbas, who called it "החלטה פוליטית, פסולה ואנטי-דמוקרטית".
  - Joint List (Hadash, Ta'al, Balad), Likud petition alleging support for Hamas's armed struggle: disqualified (tally not in Davar; a search summary gives 18-5, UNVERIFIED).
  - Ofer Cassif, Likud petition citing support for the ICJ proceedings: disqualified 19-5, 2 abstentions.
  - Voting: coalition mostly in favour; Yisrael Beiteinu in favour of all four; Yesh Atid supported only Abu Shehadeh's disqualification and did not take part in the others; The Democrats opposed disqualifying Ra'am, the Joint List and Cassif and supported disqualifying Abu Shehadeh; "Yashar" supported disqualifying Abu Shehadeh.
  - Requests to disqualify Otzma Yehudit, Religious Zionism and The Democrats were rejected.
  — [Davar, 23.09.2026](https://www.davar1.co.il/698227/); [Ynet](https://www.ynet.co.il/news/elections2026/article/hj3llhz9zx) (search summary)
- Supreme Court (nine justices, President Yitzhak Amit), early Oct 2026 (exact date not captured): overturned the disqualification of Ra'am and the Joint List unanimously; overturned Cassif's 7-2 (Justices Mintz and Stein dissenting), finding no "critical mass" of evidence. On Abu Shehadeh the justices said a majority favoured disqualification and that his words were "מכאיבים, מקוממים ומעוררי קבס"; Amit recommended he withdraw; he withdrew and the proceeding was struck. The AG and State Attorney supported disqualifying only Abu Shehadeh. — [Israel Hayom](https://www.israelhayom.co.il/news/law/article/21537301) (search summary; page 403); [Ynet](https://www.ynet.co.il/news/blogs/article/skc6ftiqfe) (search summary); [Arutz 7](https://www.inn.co.il/news/707577) (search summary); [i24NEWS](https://www.i24news.tv/he/news/israel-elections-2026/artc-ea1a4b6f) (search summary)
- Elections to the 26th Knesset: held Tuesday 27 Oct 2026 (date inferred from Haaretz of 28.10.2026 saying the Arab public voted "yesterday (Tuesday)" in large numbers despite a campaign against Arab voters). — [Haaretz, 28.10.2026](https://www.haaretz.co.il/news/elections/2026-10-28/ty-article/.highlight/000001a1-7f7e-da98-a5ad-fffed1620000) (search summary)

Ra'am's MKs include Iman Khatib-Yasin; the Joint List's include Tibi and Cassif (Odeh not on the list as extracted). 17 Arab MKs overall. — [Davar, 10.11.2026](https://www.davar1.co.il/704821/) (the extraction showed minor inconsistencies in name counts)
- Post-election: Liberman said the Arab MKs "are not an option" for the "change bloc"; debate over electing a Knesset Speaker (Matan Kahana) with Arab-party votes. — [Ynet](https://www.ynet.co.il/news/elections2026/article/rjh8kyb5gx) (search summary); [Walla, 15.11.2026](https://www.walla.co.il/news/politics/383961538)
