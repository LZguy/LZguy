# Arab Israeli political figures outside Ra'am and Hadash-Ta'al and the 7 October 2023 attack (7 Oct 2023 – 18 Nov 2026)

Method notes for the report writer:
- Quotes below were retrieved through an automated page reader. Hebrew strings are given as the reader returned them; treat them as "as reported by the cited outlet" and re-check verbatim wording before printing inside quotation marks. Where only an outlet's Hebrew/English rendering of an Arabic original was available, this is stated.
- "No statement found" means I searched (Hebrew/English/Arabic) and found nothing; it does not mean the person said nothing.
- Pages I could not open (HTTP 403/429): Israel Hayom 21456834, Makor Rishon 201574, Kan 953241, AURDIP letter. Facts from those come only from search-result snippets and are marked "snippet only".
- Background facts needed to read these notes (all from 2026 coverage): a Knesset election was held on 27 October 2026; Hadash, Ta'al and Balad ran together as a re-formed Joint List; Ra'am ran separately.

## 1. Who explicitly condemned the attack / the killing of civilians, in what words, when?

### Takeaway
Explicit condemnations on record in this scope: Mohammad Barakeh for the High Follow-Up Committee (26 Oct 2023 press conference; an early-November 2023 conference where he used the word "massacre"), former Meretz minister Issawi Frej (April 2025 podcast), and Balad chairman Sami Abu Shehadeh, whose documented explicit wording ("crimes, massacre and atrocities") dates from September 2026, during disqualification proceedings; his lawyers and the Attorney General both say he had condemned harm to civilians earlier, but I could not locate dated 2023 wording. I found no official Balad party statement from October 2023 and no 7 October statement by the Druze MKs of non-Arab parties (search gap, not evidence of silence).

### Cited Findings

**High Follow-Up Committee / Mohammad Barakeh (chair 2015–2025, former Hadash MK)**
- 26 Oct 2023, press conference (held after police cancelled a Jewish-Arab conference the Committee had planned for that day): The Seventh Eye reports Barakeh clarified that the Committee "מגנה באופן נחרץ את הפגיעה בחפים מפשע, יהודים ופלסטינים כאחד" ("firmly condemns the harm to innocents, Jews and Palestinians alike"). At the same press conference former Knesset speaker Avraham Burg said Hamas had committed "מעשים מתועבים". — [The Seventh Eye, 26 Dec 2023](https://www.the7eye.org.il/505126)
- Before 9 Nov 2023 (exact date not given), online Jewish-Arab conference with about 400 participants: Barakeh opened by saying "גם הסבל הממושך של העם הפלסטיני לא יוכל להצדיק את הטבח של ה-7 באוקטובר" ("even the prolonged suffering of the Palestinian people cannot justify the massacre of 7 October"). In a radio interview from Nazareth he said that one must not, in the name of resisting occupation, justify the killing of "the baby girl in Israel", calling it unethical. In the same appearances he said the war was against all Gaza residents, not Hamas, and cited over 10,000 Palestinians killed. Source is Zo Haderekh, the Communist Party (Maki) weekly – a partisan outlet sympathetic to Barakeh. — [Zo Haderekh, 9 Nov 2023](https://zoha.org.il/125241/)
- 25 Oct 2023, open letter by Barakeh to Israeli academic institutions: per a search snippet, he wrote that the Committee "unequivocally rejects the violations perpetrated against innocent civilians and the continuation thereof" (snippet only; page returned HTTP 429). — [AURDIP](https://aurdip.org/en/dear-academics-and-academic-institutions/)
- Mossawa Center's "100 days" report states that leaders of the Arab community "expressed their opposition to targeting civilians from the start" and that Barakeh issued a statement as Committee chair (no date/text given in the snippet; Mossawa is an Arab-rights advocacy NGO). — [Mossawa report (PDF)](https://www.mossawa.org/eng//Public/file/0100%20DAYS_%20Report%20on%20the%20Status%20of%20Palestinian-Arab%20Citizens%20of%20Israel%20During%20the%20War%20on%20Gaza.pdf)
- The Seventh Eye's "representation index" piece (with Sikkuy-Aufoq and Yifat Media Research) argues Israeli mainstream media largely ignored condemnations by Arab politicians other than Mansour Abbas; it cites Channel 14's Shai Golden saying on 15–16 Oct 2023 that Arab public figures other than Abbas had not condemned. This is the outlet's characterization. — [The Seventh Eye](https://www.the7eye.org.il/505126)

**Sami Abu Shehadeh (Balad chairman; not an MK in the 25th Knesset)**
- 10 Oct 2023, TikTok video: "Our hearts are with the families of the martyrs, the wounded, and all the families of the innocent victims" (English rendering by Arutz Sheva). The same report says the video did not name Hamas, did not condemn the massacre, and accused Israel's leadership of "racist, barbaric, and inhuman incitement ... against our people". Arutz Sheva (right-wing) attributes the report to Kan Reshet Bet radio; I did not find the Kan item itself. — [Arutz Sheva / Israel National News, 28 Sep 2026](https://www.israelnationalnews.com/news/433748)
- 19 Sep 2026, written response to N12 (Mohammad Magadli): "אני מתנגד לפשעים ולמעשי הטבח שבוצעו ב-7 באוקטובר נגד תושבי העוטף"; "עמדתי האנושית, הברורה והנחרצת נגד הפשעים שבוצעו בעוטף עזה ידועה היטב"; "מאז 7 באוקטובר הבעתי אותה פעם אחר פעם במאמרים, בראיונות ובנאומים בערבית, בעברית ובאנגלית"; "מעולם לא קראתי לאלימות, לא תמכתי באירועי 7 באוקטובר ואינני מצדיק אותם." — [N12/Mako, 19 Sep 2026](https://www.mako.co.il/news-israel-elections/2026/Article-0104778830bb0a1026.htm)
- 23 Sep 2026, Central Elections Committee hearing (by Zoom): "לא ידעתי את גודל הזוועות והקטל"; "לא הייתי צריך לכתוב את המאמר הזה"; "מעולם לא תמכתי ואינני תומך במאבק מזוין של חמאס או כל ארגון אחר." — [Ynet, 23 Sep 2026](https://www.ynet.co.il/news/elections2026/article/bjms6yb9zx); [Ynetnews](https://www.ynetnews.com/article/bkvpd7z5mx)
- 24 Sep 2026, Kan Reshet Bet radio: "מה שנעשה ב-7 באוקטובר אלה פשעים, טבח וזוועות שאני מתנגד אליהם" (reported by Arutz Sheva Hebrew). — [INN Hebrew, 24 Sep 2026](https://www.inn.co.il/news/706949)
- Third-party descriptions of earlier condemnations: Adalah (his counsel) says his statements "around 7 October and throughout the war" included explicit condemnations of harming civilians, Jews and Palestinians alike; Times of Israel reports that the Attorney General acknowledged he later condemned harm to civilians. Neither gives dates or wording. — [Adalah, 23 Sep 2026](https://www.adalah.org/he/content/view/11708); [Times of Israel, 22 Sep 2026](https://www.timesofisrael.com/ag-disqualification-of-balad-leader-from-election-should-be-seriously-considered/)
- At the Supreme Court (1 Oct 2026) his lawyer Hassan Jabareen said Abu Shehadeh had supported an international commission of inquiry into the crimes of 7 October. — [Maariv, 2 Oct 2026](https://www.maariv.co.il/news/elections-2026/article-1372478)

**Former MKs of non-Arab parties**
- Issawi Frej (former Meretz MK and Regional Cooperation Minister), 7 Apr 2025, "שמאל TALK" podcast on DemocraTV with Emily Amrousi: "מדובר בזוועה, בטבח, מדובר בכל דבר שכל בן אנוש צריך לדחות אותו"; "זה אירוע מכונן, לא פשוט לא ליהודי ולא לערבי"; "נכנסתי למצב של שוק וטראומה"; "האג'נדה שלי הפכה להיות אג'נדה שולית". He said close friends were murdered that day, naming Oded Lifshitz. — [Ice, 7 Apr 2025](https://www.ice.co.il/media/news/article/1058832)

**Arab/Druze MKs in non-Arab parties, 25th Knesset**
- Identified: Hamad Amar (Yisrael Beiteinu, Druze); Afif Abed (Likud, Druze); Akram Hasson (Druze; entered the Knesset 10 July 2025). — [Wikipedia: Hamad Amar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hamad_Amar); [Wikipedia: Akram Hasson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Akram_Hasson); [Maariv (Abed as Likud MK)](https://www.maariv.co.il/news/politics/article-1255046)
- Hamad Amar, 2025 Ynet interview on the killings of Druze in Syria's Jabal al-Druze/Sweida: "What happened in the Druze Mountain reminded me of the Holocaust"; per the search snippet he likened it to what "happened on October 7" (snippet only; a comparison invoking 7 October as a massacre, not a statement issued about 7 October). — [Ynetnews](https://www.ynetnews.com/article/f6m6r1lu8)
- Yisrael Beiteinu (Amar's party) supported the September 2026 requests to disqualify Ra'am and Abu Shehadeh; no individual statement by Amar is recorded in the sources. — [Ynet, 23 Sep 2026](https://www.ynet.co.il/news/elections2026/article/bjms6yb9zx); [Ynetnews](https://www.ynetnews.com/article/bkvpd7z5mx)

### Inferences
- Barakeh's documented wording moves from "harm to innocents, Jews and Palestinians alike" (26 Oct 2023) to an explicit "the massacre of 7 October" that "cannot be justified" (by 9 Nov 2023). Both are paired with criticism of the war in Gaza.
- For Abu Shehadeh the only contemporaneous (2023) sympathy wording found is general ("innocent victims") and does not name the attackers; his explicit "massacre/atrocities" wording is documented only from September 2026, when his candidacy was at stake. Earlier explicit condemnations are asserted by him, Adalah and (per ToI) acknowledged by the AG, but are undated in my sources.
- Druze MKs in Zionist parties are generally not the target of the claim "Arab MKs did not condemn"; the absence of search hits most likely reflects that their stance was not treated as newsworthy, but I cannot document their words.

### Gaps
- No statement found: an official Balad party communiqué dated 7–10 Oct 2023 (Arabic, Hebrew and English searches). Arab48/Balad channels should be checked directly.
- No statement found: a High Follow-Up Committee communiqué issued on 7–8 Oct 2023 itself, or any statement by the Committee of Arab Mayors on 7 October.
- No statement found: 7 October statements by Afif Abed or Akram Hasson; no direct 7 October statement by Hamad Amar from October 2023. Knesset plenary records and their social accounts were not searchable with my tools.
- No statement found for Zouheir Bahloul, Ghaida Rinawie Zoabi, Mtanes Shehadeh, Heba Yazbak, Jamal Zahalka (on 7 October specifically) or Basel Ghattas.
- Exact date of Barakeh's "cannot justify the massacre" speech is not given by the source (it predates 9 Nov 2023).

## 2. Who declined to condemn when asked, and what exactly did they answer?

### Takeaway
The one documented exchange in this scope is Sami Abu Shehadeh on Kan Reshet Bet on 24 September 2026: he called 7 October "crimes, massacre and atrocities" but refused to say the words "Hamas is a terror organization". His 10 October 2023 video is reported (by a right-wing outlet citing Kan) to contain sympathy for "innocent victims" without condemning the attack or naming Hamas.

### Cited Findings
- 24 Sep 2026, Kan Reshet Bet. Question: "האם חמאס הוא ארגון טרור?" Answer: "אני נגד טרור ואני אף פעם לא קראתי לאלימות בחיים שלי"; "אתה לא מדבר עם דובר חמאס, ואני אומר שמה שנעשה ב-7 באוקטובר אלה פשעים, טבח וזוועות שאני מתנגד אליהם." When pressed to use the exact words "ארגון טרור" he refused, saying that asking a political leader to repeat such phrases is like teaching a child. In the same interview: "המאמר שלי אחרי 7.10 היה לא מוצלח, לא הבנתי את כל התמונה"; "אם הייתי יודע את גודל הזוועות, לא הייתי כותב במילים האלה." Reported by Arutz Sheva (right-wing); the interviewer is not named. — [INN Hebrew, 24 Sep 2026](https://www.inn.co.il/news/706949)
- 10 Oct 2023 TikTok video: reported not to name Hamas, not to condemn the massacre and not to mention killings, abductions or rapes, while expressing sympathy for "all the families of the innocent victims". — [Arutz Sheva, 28 Sep 2026](https://www.israelnationalnews.com/news/433748)
- The Attorney General and State Attorney wrote that his 8 Oct 2023 article showed "sympathetic treatment, without a word of condemnation, for the 'operation'". Adalah's Hassan Jabareen replied that the AG faulted him for not condemning the attack in an article that, in Adalah's reading, was not about that and whose main message was a call for a political solution. — [Times of Israel, 22 Sep 2026](https://www.timesofisrael.com/ag-disqualification-of-balad-leader-from-election-should-be-seriously-considered/); [Adalah, 23 Sep 2026](https://www.adalah.org/he/content/view/11708)

### Inferences
- The refusal on record concerns the label "terror organization" for Hamas, not the condemnation of the 7 October acts, which he gave in the same answer.

### Gaps
- No 2023–2025 interview found in which any Balad figure, Barakeh or a former MK in this scope was asked directly to condemn and declined. Hebrew broadcast interviews from October 2023 are poorly indexed.
- I could not find the original Kan report on the 10 Oct 2023 video, nor the video itself.

## 3. Who made statements interpreted as justification or denial, exact words, and what legal or disciplinary consequences followed?

### Takeaway
Two cases: (a) Abu Shehadeh's 8 October 2023 Arabic article calling the attack an "important historic event", which led in September–October 2026 to his disqualification by the Central Elections Committee, the Attorney General's support for it, and his withdrawal on 2 October 2026 after Supreme Court justices indicated a majority would uphold it; (b) Hanin Zoabi's October 2024 Vienna speech ("those who entered on 7 October entered their own land"), for which police detained and questioned her on 21 September 2025 – no indictment found. Separately, the 9 November 2023 Nazareth detentions of Barakeh and Balad leaders concerned an anti-war vigil, not 7 October statements.

### Cited Findings

**Abu Shehadeh's 8 October 2023 article**
- Published in Arabic on 8 Oct 2023 (outlet not named in my sources). N12's Hebrew rendering: "אירוע היסטורי חשוב מבחינה צבאית, מדינית ואסטרטגית"; "אם עזה הקטנה והנצורה הצליחה לעשות זאת, וברמה הזאת, אחרים יכולים לעשות זאת בצורה יותר קלה". — [N12/Mako, 19 Sep 2026](https://www.mako.co.il/news-israel-elections/2026/Article-0104778830bb0a1026.htm)
- Times of Israel's English rendering: "this is a historic event of significant military, political, and strategic importance"; the attack was "entirely consistent with a law of physics we all know: that great pressure leads to explosion"; Israel's intelligence services "are human beings like us"; "it is possible to prevent them from penetrating our countries, our parties, and our political movements"; "If the small, besieged Gaza managed to do this, and at this level, then others can do so more effectively and easily"; reference to the "diplomatic fruits of this operation". — [Times of Israel, 22 Sep 2026](https://www.timesofisrael.com/ag-disqualification-of-balad-leader-from-election-should-be-seriously-considered/)
- The 10 Oct 2023 video reportedly repeated a line from the article: "The Palestinian issue has undergone a significant political and strategic event." — [Arutz Sheva, 28 Sep 2026](https://www.israelnationalnews.com/news/433748)
- Mid-Sep 2026: Otzma Yehudit / Itamar Ben Gvir petitioned to disqualify him, citing the article and other statements. Hadash-Ta'al said it had not seen the article before and pressed him to retract; his office said he had forgotten the article until it was attached to the petition. — [N12/Mako](https://www.mako.co.il/news-israel-elections/2026/Article-0104778830bb0a1026.htm)
- 22 Sep 2026: AG Gali Baharav-Miara and State Attorney Amit Aisman told the Committee disqualification "should be seriously considered"; they rejected his claim of not knowing the scale, noting hundreds were known dead by 8 Oct. At the hearing the AG said there was "בסיס לפסילה" and called his explanations "מיתממות". — [Times of Israel](https://www.timesofisrael.com/ag-disqualification-of-balad-leader-from-election-should-be-seriously-considered/); [Ynet](https://www.ynet.co.il/news/elections2026/article/bjms6yb9zx)
- 23 Sep 2026: Central Elections Committee disqualified him. Vote reported as 31–4 by Adalah and Ynet, and as 30–4 on each of two grounds by Maariv. Chair Justice Noam Sohlberg departed from the custom of abstaining and voted in favour: "החלטתי לחרוג ממנהג זה כי לא מצאתי מענה שיכול להניח את הדעת". Yesh Atid's representatives voted for his disqualification (while abstaining on Ra'am and the Joint List); Yashar! (Eisenkot), Blue and White and Yisrael Beiteinu backed it; Yair Golan (Democrats) said the remarks crossed "a democratic red line" while opposing disqualification of whole lists. Ahmad Tibi defended him. Balad called the petition political persecution. — [Adalah](https://www.adalah.org/he/content/view/11708); [Ynet](https://www.ynet.co.il/news/elections2026/article/bjms6yb9zx); [Maariv](https://www.maariv.co.il/news/elections-2026/article-1372478); [Ynetnews](https://www.ynetnews.com/article/bkvpd7z5mx); [INN](https://www.inn.co.il/news/706500)
- Same day the Committee disqualified Ra'am (18–5–1), the Joint List (18–5) and Ofer Cassif (18–5–2 per Adalah; 19–5–2 per Maariv); the AG opposed those three disqualifications. A request against Balad as a party (by "Orot HaShahar") was rejected unanimously on procedural grounds. — [Adalah](https://www.adalah.org/he/content/view/11708); [Ynet](https://www.ynet.co.il/news/elections2026/article/bjms6yb9zx)
- AG's written position to the Supreme Court: "יש לאשר את פסילתו של המועמד לכנסת סמי אבו שחאדה". His reply conceded "הוא טעה כשפרסמו במיוחד בעיתוי זה" and described the article as analysis calling for diplomatic action. Adalah said the AG's position "מעמיקה את משטר האפרטהייד נגד האזרחים הערבים בישראל". — [Ynet](https://www.ynet.co.il/news/elections2026/article/rj00hxjkqzl)
- 1 Oct 2026, Supreme Court, nine justices. AG's representative Yonatan Berman: "קשה לראות בדברים פרשנות ניטרלית למעשים". Jabareen argued mistranslation from Arabic; Justice Willner asked why Abu Shehadeh had not raised this earlier; President Amit (reported to read Arabic) said the translation of a disputed word in a 2022 article was correct. Abu Shehadeh: "הכוונה שלי הייתה דווקא אזהרה למדינת ישראל"; "במאמר יש דברים לא נכונים". — [Maariv, 2 Oct 2026](https://www.maariv.co.il/news/elections-2026/article-1372478)
- 2 Oct 2026: he withdrew his candidacy after the justices recommended it and indicated a majority for upholding the disqualification; Balad said he remains party chairman. The Court overturned the disqualifications of Ra'am, the Joint List and Cassif (Cassif by 7–2, Justices Mintz and Stein dissenting, per search summaries of i24/Al Jazeera). Al-Monitor reports all judges agreed his statements were "painful, disturbing and revolting". His reaction: "in the Jewish state, there are two different laws for citizens according to their identity." Ben Gvir said his party "managed to oust" him. — [Hebrew Wikipedia](https://he.wikipedia.org/wiki/הבחירות_לכנסת_העשרים_ושש); [Al-Monitor, 2 Oct 2026](https://www.al-monitor.com/originals/2026/10/israeli-arab-leader-quits-election-under-pressure-court-over-october-7-remarks); [Al Jazeera, 2 Oct 2026](https://aljazeera.com/news/2026/10/2/israels-supreme-court-overturns-election-panel-ban-on-arab-parties); [i24](https://www.i24news.tv/he/news/israel-elections-2026/artc-9e4e4a93)
- Critical framing by some outlets: The National and Al Jazeera described a "double standard" in that far-right candidates were not barred; Adalah made the same argument. — [The National, 24 Sep 2026](https://www.thenationalnews.com/news/mena/2026/09/24/arab-parties-barred-from-israels-elections-but-not-the-far-right/); [Al Jazeera, 3 Oct 2026](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/10/3/israel-election-double-standard-arab-leader-forced-out-far-right-cleared)

**Hanin Zoabi (former Balad MK)**
- Speech at the "Palestine Congress" in Vienna, 5–6 Oct 2024 (venue per Ice, snippet only). Quotes as reported by Walla: "אלה שנכנסו בשבעה באוקטובר, הם נכנסו לארץ שלהם"; "זה לא חמאס שהתנגד, זה העם הפלסטיני". - UNVERIFIED (snippet only, page blocked): Makor Rishon (right-leaning) headline "'ישראל מבצעת רצח עם': חנין זועבי המשיכה להסית בחקירתה"; a search summary says she told interrogators Israel is committing genocide and that 7 October "had to take place". I could not open the article to confirm the wording or its source (apparently a leaked interrogation account). — [Makor Rishon](https://www.makorrishon.co.il/news/law/article/201574)

**Nazareth detentions, 9 November 2023**
- Police detained Barakeh (on his way to the vigil, in an unmarked vehicle per Adalah) and, at the square, Balad chairman Sami Abu Shehadeh, former Balad chairman Mtanes Shehadeh, former MK Hanin Zoabi and Balad deputy secretary-general Yousef Tatour. Outlets differ on the count (Ynet: four former MKs; Ahram: six leaders). Barakeh had said the vigil would have no more than 50 people; it came a day after a Supreme Court ruling on protest bans. — [Adalah](https://www.adalah.org/en/content/view/10955); [Ynet](https://www.ynet.co.il/news/article/rkikom5xa); [The New Arab](https://www.newarab.com/news/israeli-police-detains-former-arab-mks-anti-war-event); [Ahram](https://english.ahram.org.eg/News/511914.aspx)
- Police: the detainees "ניסו להסית ולהפר את הסדר הציבורי"; "משטרת ישראל תנקוט ביד קשה כנגד כל מי שינסה להפר את הסדר הציבורי"; the protest "עלולה להוביל להסתה ולפגיעה בציבור". — [Ynet](https://www.ynet.co.il/news/article/rkikom5xa); [Ynet](https://www.ynet.co.il/news/article/ryc6lzcxp)
- Barakeh was released after more than six hours; the others that evening, barred from Nazareth for up to 14 days (per Adalah). Barakeh: "המעצר שלי היום היה כמו פעולת קומנדו. הם רוצים להשתיק את הקול שלנו". ACRI, Mossawa, Hadash and Givat Haviva criticized the detentions. No source ties these detentions to statements about 7 October. — [Adalah](https://www.adalah.org/en/content/view/10955); [Ynet](https://www.ynet.co.il/news/article/rkikom5xa)

**High Follow-Up Committee leadership change**
- Barakeh chaired the Committee from 2015 to 2025. Former Balad MK Jamal Zahalka won the chairmanship in 2025 (The New Arab: "elected in November"; three months in office as of 24 Feb 2026). ## 4. Self-contradictions (both statements, with dates)

### Takeaway
The clearest documented tension is Abu Shehadeh's: an 8 October 2023 article describing the attack as an important historic event whose "diplomatic fruits" should be pursued, versus September 2026 statements that he never supported the events, opposes the "crimes and massacre", and "should not have written" the article. His explanation that he did not know the scale was rejected by the Attorney General and challenged by a reported 10 October 2023 video repeating the theme.

### Cited Findings
- Statement A, 8 Oct 2023 (article, Arabic; translations): "אירוע היסטורי חשוב מבחינה צבאית, מדינית ואסטרטגית"; "If the small, besieged Gaza managed to do this ... then others can do so more effectively and easily"; "diplomatic fruits of this operation". — [N12/Mako](https://www.mako.co.il/news-israel-elections/2026/Article-0104778830bb0a1026.htm); [Times of Israel](https://www.timesofisrael.com/ag-disqualification-of-balad-leader-from-election-should-be-seriously-considered/)
- Statement B, 19–24 Sep 2026: "מעולם לא קראתי לאלימות, לא תמכתי באירועי 7 באוקטובר ואינני מצדיק אותם"; "אילו הייתי מודע לממדי הזוועות, לא הייתי מפרסם אותו"; "לא הייתי צריך לכתוב את המאמר הזה"; "המאמר שלי אחרי 7.10 היה לא מוצלח, לא הבנתי את כל התמונה". — [N12/Mako](https://www.mako.co.il/news-israel-elections/2026/Article-0104778830bb0a1026.htm); [Ynet](https://www.ynet.co.il/news/elections2026/article/bjms6yb9zx); [INN](https://www.inn.co.il/news/706949)
- Challenges to the explanation: the AG noted hundreds were known dead by 8 Oct 2023; Ben Gvir at the hearing: "יש מישהו בישראל שלא ידע מה היו הממדים?"; Arutz Sheva (citing Kan) reported that the 10 Oct 2023 video repeated the article's theme three days after the attack. — [Times of Israel](https://www.timesofisrael.com/ag-disqualification-of-balad-leader-from-election-should-be-seriously-considered/); [Ynet](https://www.ynet.co.il/news/elections2026/article/bjms6yb9zx); [Arutz Sheva](https://www.israelnationalnews.com/news/433748)
- Shifting explanations within September–October 2026: (i) did not know the scale (19–23 Sep); (ii) the article was analysis whose main call was diplomatic (reply to the AG); (iii) translation from Arabic distorted meanings, raised by counsel at the Supreme Court on 1 Oct, prompting Justice Willner's question why this had not been raised before; (iv) "the intention was actually a warning to the State of Israel" (1 Oct). — [Ynet](https://www.ynet.co.il/news/elections2026/article/rj00hxjkqzl); [Maariv](https://www.maariv.co.il/news/elections-2026/article-1372478)
- Barakeh: no contradiction found; his recorded statements consistently pair condemnation of harming innocents / the "massacre of 7 October" with opposition to the Gaza war. — [The Seventh Eye](https://www.the7eye.org.il/505126); [Zo Haderekh](https://zoha.org.il/125241/)

### Inferences
- Whether Statement A amounts to "support" is exactly what was disputed: the AG, the Committee majority (including its chair) and, by reported indication, a Supreme Court majority read it as support for armed struggle; Abu Shehadeh, Adalah and Balad read it as analysis. The report should present both readings with attribution.
- For the claim under examination ("Arab MKs did not condemn"): Abu Shehadeh and Zoabi were not MKs in the 25th Knesset, and Barakeh is a former MK; statements by them are sometimes attributed to "Arab MKs" in public debate, which the report may want to flag.

### Gaps
- Without the dated 2023–2025 condemnations that Abu Shehadeh says he made "in Arabic, Hebrew and English", it cannot be established from my sources whether his explicit condemnation predates September 2026.
- No other self-contradictions were identified for figures in this scope, but coverage of Zoabi, Zahalka, Mtanes Shehadeh and the Druze MKs is thin.
